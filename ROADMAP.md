# Roadmap

## Next

- Play a campaign past the first battle with real input; seed whatever the
  logged mid-function returns point at.
- Listen to the audio in a windowed run.
- Two-player modes (head to head, side by side).
- Drop seeds as genrecomp's analyzer learns base-plus-offset tables (its
  ROADMAP).

## Deferred

- Linux and macOS: genrecomp builds there, but Setup and play are
  Windows-only for now.

## Out of scope

- Distributing the ROM, generated source, or builds that include game data.
- Commercial use (Genesis Plus GX's licence).
